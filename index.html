About this atlas. Compiled 4–15 September 2026 and updated 13 October 2026 as a starting point for enquiry, not a substitute for contacting an organisation; inclusion does not imply endorsement. Entries marked verified were confirmed against a 2025–26 organisation or news source; reported entries rest on organisation reports or a known permanent programme; unverified entries derive from the 2016 census of surgical NGOs (Ng-Kamstra et al., World J Surg 2016) and may be out of date.

Sources. Organisation websites and mission reports; the 2016 Ng-Kamstra census; the G4 Alliance member directory; Vervoort et al. 2021 (cardiac surgery NGOs); national professional-society registers (SICPRE, SECPRE, Académie Nationale de Chirurgie, BLÄK, NOV, RACS). Map: Natural Earth via world-atlas (public domain); boundaries and names follow that dataset and do not imply a position on any territorial dispute.

Corrections and additions are welcome — please contact the compiler.
