# Surgical Outreach Atlas

An interactive world map of organisations providing surgical outreach — visiting teams, permanent hospitals and programmes, training partnerships and funders — listed by country, with the type of surgery, type of outreach, most recent dated visit and main locations for each organisation.

Live site: https://jaredmcs.github.io/surgicalatlas/

The atlas is a single self-contained page (`index.html`): no server, no external requests. It was compiled 4–15 September 2026 and updated 13 October 2026 from organisation websites and mission reports, the 2016 census of surgical NGOs (Ng-Kamstra et al., *World J Surg*), the G4 Alliance directory, Vervoort et al. 2021 (cardiac surgery NGOs) and national professional-society registers. It is a starting point for enquiry, not a substitute for contacting an organisation; inclusion does not imply endorsement. Corrections and additions are welcome — please open an issue.

Map: Natural Earth via [world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas) (public domain).
